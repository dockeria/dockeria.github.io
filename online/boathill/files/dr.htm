Kubrick's direction was nothing short of genius. The film is laced with so many subtle innuendoes and ironies; one could see it twenty times and still not find them all. For instance, most of the names had sexual connotations. Strangelove (perversion), Mandrake (a root to used to promote conception), Merkin (slang for female genitalia) Muffley (same), Buck (stud) Turgidson (distended with swelling). The battle to take over the air force complex took place with a sign that said, "Peace Is Our Profession" in the background. Then there is President Muffley's great line, "You can't fight in here, this is the War Room!" - Mike Svinis
