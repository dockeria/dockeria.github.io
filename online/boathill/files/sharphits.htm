Cat's Eye Low Lux
The Sharp advanced "Cat's Eye" circuit allows recording down to under 1 Lux (the light of one candle about one yard away from the subject) and eliminates complex lighting arrangements and harsh flood light glare so you can capture images in almost no light.

Titler/Title Writer
Titler lets you superimpose one of 8-preset titles on your videos when recording. Title Writer allows you to customize your own titles with up to 15 characters.

Touch Control Panel LCD View Screen
The world's first touch control panel LCD view screen allows you to zoom, focus and control brightness while recording. Even in playback, just touch the screen to zoom in on any part of the image.

i.LINK™ Cable Capabilities
For incredibly high-speed (up to 400 Mbps) transfer of full motion video from digital camcorders to computers or other i.LINK capable camcorders, nothing comes close to an i.LINK high-speed cable transfer. And, since there is no need to convert digital data into analog, there is no loss of image quality. It is the ideal vehicle for transmitting digital camcorder data.

Picture in Picture (PIP)
With the Twincam's picture in picture function, the view screen displays the zoom lens view within the wide angle lens view. Now you can see a close-up detail of your wide shot.

Special Effects
Choose from a variety of "picture perfect" digital effects such as Fade, Black & White, Sepia, and more, to add your own personal creativity to any scene.

Digital Gamma Correction
The new backlight compensation adjusts only the dark parts of a shot to optimal brightness levels, and leaves the bright sections untouched, so they don't become glaringly washed out.

High Resolution Color LCD View Screen
The 4" color LCD view screen has a viewing area that is 77% larger than the 3" view screen. It features a viewing area of 7.57 square inches with 112,320 pixels.

The 3" color LCD view screen features a viewing area of 4.27 square inches with 89,856 pixels.

 Digital Image Stabilization (D.I.S.)
The D.I.S. circuit senses movement of the user holding the camera and helps to reduce image shake and jitter, delivering a more stable picture.

The Auto LCD Brightness Control function automatically changes the backlight brightness level of the LCD screen to match lighting conditions.
